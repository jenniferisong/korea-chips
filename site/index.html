Korea Chip Trade Tracker

Since 2022 the US has restricted chips and chipmaking tools bound for China. South Korea makes most of the world's memory chips and runs big factories inside China. This page tracks, month by month, where Korea's chips actually go and whether China's own records agree.

        How this works

        Every week a GitHub Action downloads the latest monthly figures from the UN Comtrade database, recomputes everything and republishes this page. Countries report to the UN with a lag of several months to a year, so the newest months fill in over time.

        • Products use 6-digit HS codes: 848620 and 848690 (chipmaking equipment and parts), 854231, 854232 and 854239 (processors, memory and other chips). Cosmetics (330499) and auto parts (870899) are a comparison group not covered by the controls.
        • Southeast Asia is Vietnam, Singapore and Malaysia. Taiwan appears in Comtrade as "Other Asia, nes".
        • The mirror gap compares two records of the same trade. Korea logs exports by first destination; China logs imports by country of origin. Goods routed through Hong Kong or Singapore count as "to Hong Kong" in Korea's books and "from Korea" in China's, so the ratio rises when indirect routing grows. It is a signal to investigate, not proof of evasion: timing, valuation and classification differences also move it.
        • All values are 12-month rolling sums. China reports January and February together.
